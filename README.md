# The Vibe Awards · Best of Yamhill County nominations

Public nomination page for The Vibe Awards, Best of Yamhill County 2027, presented by YamCo Vibe.

Sip. Savor. Explore.

This is a static site (`index.html` and `assets/`) with no build step. Nominations are stored in the YamCo Vibe app database. The page reads the nomination open and close dates from the database, so changing those dates does not require a redeploy. Awards on the form are grouped by section (Food & Drink, Shop Local, Stay & Explore). Coffee, Bakery, and Restaurants are separate awards. Farm Stand/Market and Hidden Gem are official awards.
Shareable “I Nominated” and “I Voted” badges live in `assets/`. The nomination thank-you screen has a Share to Facebook button (Facebook’s sharer dialog only — nothing is posted automatically) and a download-image option.

After DNS is pointed at this site, the page will live at [nominate.yamcovibe.com](https://nominate.yamcovibe.com).

Optional URL parameters:

- `?award=wineries` (or another award slug) preselects an award
- `?contest=best-of-2027` selects a contest (this is the default)
